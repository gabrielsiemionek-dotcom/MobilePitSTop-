  <h3>Machine Polishing FAQs</h3>

  <div class="mps-acc" id="mpolFAQs">
    <details>
      <summary>Will it remove every scratch?<span class="plus"></span></summary>
      <div class="content">No. Polishing removes a tiny amount of clearcoat to level the surface around a defect &mdash; so marks <strong>within</strong> the clearcoat come out.     </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Is machine polishing safe for my paint?<span class="plus"></span></summary>
      <div class="content">Done properly, yes &mdash; which is why we <strong>measure the clearcoat</strong> and polish a test section first. Done badly it burns through edges and leaves holograms. That&rsquo;s the difference between a machine and a machine in the right hands.</div>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>How do I know if I need 1-stage or multi-stage?<span class="plus"></span></summary>
      <div class="content">Look at the car in direct sunlight. Fine cobwebbing and swirls that appear as circles around a light source &mdash; <strong>1-stage</strong>. Deep straight scratches, heavy marring or a dull, hazy finish that doesn&rsquo;t change with the light &mdash; <strong>multi-stage</strong>. Send us a photo in daylight and we&rsquo;ll say honestly; most cars are the 1-stage.</div>
    </details>
    <details>
      <summary>Why does the multi-stage have to stay overnight?<span class="plus"></span></summary>
      <div class="content">It needs controlled lighting, a stable temperature and more hours than a driveway gives. The car comes to our unit in <strong>Birkenhead</strong>, stays inside overnight, and we tell you when it&rsquo;s ready.</div>

          setTimeout(function(){ target.classList.remove('mps-flash'); }, 1600);
        });
      });

    /* -- Accordions: only one open at a time -- */
    var allSummaries = Array.prototype.slice.call(document.querySelectorAll(
      '#learnMoreMachinePolishing .mps-acc details summary, #mpolFAQs details summary'
    ));
    allSummaries.forEach(function(summary){
      summary.addEventListener('click', function(){
        var thisDetails = summary.parentElement;
        allSummaries.forEach(function(other){
          var od = other.parentElement;
          if(od !== thisDetails && od.open) od.open = false;
        });
      });
    });
  }

  if(document.readyState === 'loading'){
    document.addEventListener('DOMContentLoaded', init);
  } else {
    setTimeout(init, 0);
  }

})();
</script>
