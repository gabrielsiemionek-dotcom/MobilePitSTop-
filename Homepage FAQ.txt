<section class="mps-faq">
  <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
  <p class="mps-faq-sub">Anything we haven&rsquo;t covered, call or text <a href="tel:+447592196929">07592&nbsp;196929</a> &mdash; we answer quickly.</p>

  <details>
    <summary>What areas do you cover?</summary>
    <p>We cover <strong>Cheshire, Merseyside and Flintshire</strong> &mdash; roughly a 20 mile radius of Ellesmere Port, and travel is free within it. That includes Chester, Liverpool, Birkenhead, Wallasey, Runcorn, Northwich, Crewe, St Helens, Mold, Flint, Connah&rsquo;s Quay and the surrounding towns.</p>
    <p>Slightly outside? Message us and we&rsquo;ll tell you straight whether it works.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>How much does a valet cost?</summary>
    <p>Mini Valet from <strong>&pound;50</strong>, Interior or Exterior Deep Clean from <strong>&pound;60</strong>, Full Valet from <strong>&pound;100</strong>, and our Premium package from <strong>&pound;180</strong>. Odour removal starts at <strong>&pound;120</strong>, and paintwork services &mdash; machine polishing and ceramic coating &mdash; start at <strong>&pound;275</strong>.</p>
    <p>Every price depends on vehicle size. Use the questionnaire on any service page and you&rsquo;ll see your exact total, including any add-ons, before you book.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>Do you offer a maintenance plan?</summary>
    <p>Yes, and it&rsquo;s worth having if you want the car kept right rather than rescued twice a year. Start with a Full Valet to reset the vehicle, we agree how often suits it, then you get a discount code worth <strong>up to 30% off every visit</strong> &mdash; 30% weekly, 25% every 2&ndash;3 weeks, 20% every 4&ndash;6 weeks.</p>
    <p>Every plan visit also includes <strong>free chemical decontamination</strong>. No contract and no direct debit &mdash; change or stop whenever you like. <a href="https://mobilepitstop.uk/car-detailing-maintenance-plan">See how the plan works</a>.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>Do I need to be home while you work?</summary>
    <p>No. Plenty of customers leave the keys and get on with their day, or book us while they&rsquo;re at work. We just need access to the vehicle, somewhere to park alongside it, and a plug socket.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>Do I need to provide water or electricity?</summary>
    <p>Electricity only &mdash; a standard 13A household socket, and we carry a 45m extension lead. We bring our own water supply, so no outside tap is needed.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>How long does a valet take?</summary>
    <p>It depends on the package and vehicle size. Mini Valets take around <strong>1 hour</strong>, Full Valets <strong>3&ndash;6 hours</strong>, and an Interior Deep Clean <strong>3&ndash;5 hours</strong>. Paint enhancement and ceramic coating are usually a <strong>full day</strong>.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>What&rsquo;s the difference between valeting and detailing?</summary>
    <p>A valet cleans the car. Detailing corrects the paintwork itself &mdash; decontamination, clay bar and machine polishing to remove swirl marks and light scratches &mdash; and then protects it.</p>
    <p>If the paint looks dull or cobwebbed in direct sunlight, that&rsquo;s detailing. If it just looks dirty, you want a valet.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>Can you remove tough stains, pet hair and odours?</summary>
    <p>Yes. Stains are handled with steam, shampoo and wet-vac extraction, followed by high-heat drying so the car comes back touch dry rather than damp. Severe pet hair needs specialist tools and focused air agitation, which is a separate add-on.</p>
    <p>For smells that survive a clean &mdash; cigarette, spilt milk, bio &mdash; we remove the source first, then treat the cabin with ozone or chlorine dioxide. <a href="https://mobilepitstop.uk/smoke/milk/bio-odour-removal">See odour removal</a>.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>How long does a ceramic coating last?</summary>
    <p>Our coating is rated for around <strong>12 months</strong> with sensible maintenance &mdash; hand washing rather than automatic car washes. It goes on over corrected paintwork, so it seals in a polished finish rather than sealing in swirl marks.</p>
    <p>It won&rsquo;t stop stone chips, and we&rsquo;d be wary of anyone who tells you otherwise. What it does is resist chemicals, hold gloss and make the car far easier to clean.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>Do you work in bad weather?</summary>
    <p>Light rain is usually fine for interior work. For extreme conditions we&rsquo;ll either reschedule or invite you to our garage in Birkenhead. Machine polishing and ceramic coating both need a dry surface, so those are more weather-dependent.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>Are your products safe on my paint and interior?</summary>
    <p>Yes. We use professional-grade, pH-appropriate products that are paint-safe and interior-safe, and a safe wash method &mdash; snow foam first, clean mitts, no shared sponges. That last part matters more than the products: grit carried between cars is what puts swirl marks into paint.</p>
  </details>

  <details>
    <summary>How do I book, and how do I pay?</summary>
    <p>Book instantly online through any service page, or call/text <a href="tel:+447592196929">07592&nbsp;196929</a>. You&rsquo;ll pick your options, see the exact price, and choose a slot on our live calendar.</p>
    <p>A <strong>10% deposit</strong> secures the booking, with the balance taken after the job &mdash; cash, contactless, bank transfer, or we can provide a professional invoice.</p>
  </details>

</section>
